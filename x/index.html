Why this is the problem worth spending on

Four numbers, each from a public source. None of them is mine.

  • 54% of Stripe-verified products on Indie Hackers earn exactly nothing. The rate has not moved since 2023, even though AI tools made shipping roughly five times faster. Building got cheap. Being found did not. Solo Operator Stack analysis of Stripe-verified Indie Hackers revenue, 2026
  • 86% to 90% of Chrome extensions have fewer than 1,000 users, and about 0.12% have ever passed one million. Shipping into a marketplace is not distribution. It is a queue. DebugBear extension census and chrome-stats.com, September 2026
  • 8% instead of 15% is how often someone clicks any normal search result once Google puts an AI Overview on top, and 1% click a source inside the overview. The free traffic that used to arrive by accident is going away. Pew Research Center, about 69,000 tracked searches, reported by Search Engine Land
  • 60% of search referral traffic is what small publishers lost over two years, against 22% for large ones. Being small is now its own ranking penalty, so the machinery has to be better, not just present. Chartbeat data in the Reuters Institute 2026 trends report, via Press Gazette

Marc Andreessen puts it this way: the top reason his firm passes on founders it otherwise likes is that they focus on the product to the exclusion of everything else. Peter Thiel is blunter. If you have invented something new but not an effective way to sell it, you have a bad business, however good the product is. I am not going to pretend distribution guarantees sales. It does not. What it does is decide whether you get a chance at all.

Before you buy

Do I need all three distribution products?

No, and I would rather you did not buy three things at once. They sit at different points of the same problem. aiwebsitepipeline decides how a site is built, toolsthatrank fills it with pages, handsofflinks earns the links that make those pages rank. If you already have a site you are happy with, skip the first. If you already have pages nobody links to, start with the last. Buy the one that matches where you are stuck, use it, then decide.

Which one should I buy first?

Start from the problem, not the price. Pages exist but nothing ranks, and no site links to you, buy handsofflinks. A model has invented a statistic on a page you shipped, buy toolsthatrank. You are about to start site number four and dread re-making the same fifty decisions, buy aiwebsitepipeline. Your agentic dev runs have no gates and no budget ceiling, buy ml0x Pipeline. A training run just failed and you are debugging rather than learning, buy Training Run Triage. If two of those describe you, buy the cheaper one and see whether you actually use it.

Has any of this made you money?

The machinery produces traffic and I can show you the number. Across 43 properties in my Search Console account, the last 28 days brought 446,585 impressions and 1,833 clicks from Google. What that traffic has not yet done is convert well, and two of the five products on this page have sold nothing at all. Both of their pages say so in those words. You are buying tools I use on my own sites, with the results printed next to them, including the results that went against me.

What do I need to run these?

Your own machine, and for toolsthatrank and ml0x Pipeline your own API keys, because you pay your own inference. handsofflinks is macOS only and needs Node 18 or newer. aiwebsitepipeline is Python with the standard library only and needs no key. Training Run Triage needs Python 3.9 or newer, with torch only where it cannot be avoided. ml0x Pipeline is bash and Python. None of the five is a hosted service and none of them phones home.

What happens if it does not work for me?

You ask for the money back inside the window that product states and you get it. No licence key exists to revoke on any of the five, so nothing stops working while you wait. Reply to the Stripe receipt, which carries my support address and reaches me directly. If a formula or a figure in any of them turns out to be wrong, I would rather hear about it than not.

Are these subscriptions?

No. Every one of the five is a single payment and nothing renews. The refund windows are deliberately different, because each product site sets its own and I have not flattened them into one number. handsofflinks is 30 days and unconditional. toolsthatrank is 14 days in full. aiwebsitepipeline is 60 days with no reason needed, and the two sold here on ml0x.com are also 60 days, no questions asked.
